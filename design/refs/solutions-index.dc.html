Questions

Frequently asked

What data quality problems does Pearstop solve?

Hard services, construction, infrastructure, and manufacturing companies share a common challenge: operational data that is decentralised, inconsistent, and difficult to use across teams and systems. Poor procurement data quality, unreliable asset registers, and unclassified spend are the most common blockers to category management, predictive maintenance, and digital transformation. Pearstop specialises in cleaning and structuring this operational data so technical businesses can act on it.

Which solution should I start with?

Start with the problem in front of you. If invoices sit unread, start with extraction. If spend is coded by hand or not at all, start with UNSPSC classification. If you need a baseline for a tender or savings target, start with spend visibility. Most teams move through these in that order.

Does our data need to be clean first?

No. Cleaning the data is the first thing Pearstop does. Send invoices, ERP exports, portal downloads or spreadsheets in whatever state they are in.

Can Pearstop tell us whether a price is reasonable?

Yes. Every line gets a standard label, so the same item can be compared across suppliers, sites and providers. You can see whether a price sits in the normal range before you approve it. Asset owners use this to check the spend their FM provider manages for them.

Which industries does Pearstop work with?

Facilities management, hard services, cleaning, construction, infrastructure and manufacturing. Also asset owners who outsource FM, and procurement consultancies who run spend analysis for their clients.

How accurate is automated spend classification?

Around 95% of lines are classified automatically. The rest are flagged and routed to a person, so accuracy stays high without every line needing a manual check.

How long does it take?

A first sample of 200 lines usually comes back within a few working days. After that, classification runs continuously as new invoices arrive.
