The situation

One manufacturer, a dozen spellings

SPIE Building Solutions manages a large portfolio of building assets across many sites. Over the years its register was filled from different maintenance systems, contractor uploads and manual entry.

The result: the same manufacturer under a dozen spellings, and equipment types missing or recorded wrongly. The lists worked for a mechanic on site. They did not work for planning maintenance or pricing bid risk across a portfolio.

Why this is our problem too

Every asset carries a manufacturer and a type, the same way every purchase order line carries a supplier and a description. The engine that cleans spend data works on an asset register without changes.

How it worked

Matched against the list SPIE already trusts

Early on we saw general AI models guess or invent supplier names. So every row was matched against SPIE's own approved supplier list, and nothing else.

What we learned

When a client has an approved list, match against that list

Matching only against SPIE's validated suppliers raised both accuracy and the team's trust in the output. It is now standard on every Pearstop engagement with a defined supplier base.

What it changes

From servicing assets to advising on them

With a structured register, SPIE can look at a whole equipment family and spot repeat failures across a client's portfolio. A technician can say:

“This pump family fails at this rate across your sites. Here is what we recommend.”
