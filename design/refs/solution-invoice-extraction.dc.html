The problem

Why spend data stays unusable months after the invoice arrives

Every supplier lays out an invoice differently. The same supplier changes layout depending on the depot or system that sent it. Plenty still arrive as a scan or a photo of a paper delivery note. Someone has to read each one before anything can be categorised, compared or reported on. At volume, that step becomes the permanent bottleneck.

Unread in the inbox

Nobody has time to open invoices, let alone key them in.

One supplier, many layouts

The layout changes with whichever depot or system generated it.

A cycle behind

Entry backlogs push classification and reporting a full cycle behind actual spend.

Stale on arrival

By the time a line is typed, checked and corrected, its number is out of date.
